Read the privacy policy

  • Never sold, never used for ads

    We do not sell personal information, and we do not use uploaded video for advertising.

  • Only for your team

    Video is used only to produce analysis for your team. It is not shared with other teams or made public.

  • Yours to delete

    You can delete your video and your account at any time.

Before you film, make sure you have permission from your club, school, or the players' guardians.

Goodto know.

What video works best?

Any phone video of the full game, recorded from the stands. Try to keep as much of the court in frame as you can.

How do I delete my video or account?

Email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which sports are supported?

Renshu is starting with youth basketball in Tokyo.
