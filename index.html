BEFORE YOU START

A few things
worth knowing.

Can I use my own footage?

The pilot lets coaches add recordings, review moments, and build practice plans. Contact us to discuss access and the footage you have permission to use.

Does Renshu make the coaching decisions?

No. Detection helps surface person and ball candidates. You review identities, confirm observations, and choose the coaching focus. Automatic detections do not become confirmed statistics.

Where is my work saved?

The pilot supports a local browser workspace and configured private accounts. Video files stay separate from session exports. We will explain the storage setup before you join; see our privacy policy for the current policy.

Can my whole staff share a workspace?

Each account has a private workspace. Shared staff membership is not available yet. You can export a session or practice plan to share it deliberately.
